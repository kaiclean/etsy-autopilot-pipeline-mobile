## Summary
- Blueprint for an 'AI social media team' covering research, ads, content, posting, leads, sales calls, follow-ups and reviews, with human sign-off before anything goes live.
- Ads: scans competitor ads that have run for weeks (proven winners), Claude writes 20 variants, Higgsfield makes video from product photos, ElevenLabs voices; 48 h test, then budget shifts to winners within a cap.
- Leads: reads comments/DMs on IG/TikTok/FB, tags buying intent ('how much?', 'ship to Canada?') and sends a link.
- Sales: an AI voice agent calls back within 60 s; attribution from ad to comment to call; one-page summary every morning.

## Key takeaways
- Long-running competitor ads are a free signal of what converts.
- Speed-to-lead matters: answer buyer-intent comments immediately.
- Approval gate + budget caps make automation safe.
- A daily one-page summary that ties actions to customers, not followers.
