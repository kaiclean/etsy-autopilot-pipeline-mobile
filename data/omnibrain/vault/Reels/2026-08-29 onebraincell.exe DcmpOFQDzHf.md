# AuDHD burnout isn't always fixed by simply getting more rest

## Caption
AuDHD burnout isn't always fixed by simply getting more rest.

Sometimes you can sleep, stay home, cancel everything, and still wake up feeling completely drained.

You stop replying to people.
Plans start feeling overwhelming.
Even the things you normally enjoy can suddenly feel like another responsibility.

And the hardest part is needing two opposite things at once.

You need stimulation, but everything feels overwhelming.

You need connection, but being around people feels exhausting.

You need rest, but too much isolation can leave you feeling even worse.

So you push yourself to function like normal.

Then your brain crashes.

And the cycle starts again.

Recovery may not mean finding the energy to do more.

Sometimes it means removing as much pressure as possible, keeping life familiar, and giving yourself permission to function differently for a while.

You don't always need to push harder.
Sometimes your brain needs less to carry.

## Summary
- AuDHD burnout often isn't fixed by rest: people rest, but don't feel rested, stop replying, and even hobbies feel like work.
- A conflicting pull: need stimulation but it's exhausting; need rest but isolation makes it worse; push, then crash, and the cycle repeats.
- What helps: do much less, drop non-essentials, keep 'one safe person, one familiar place, one small thing that feels good'.
