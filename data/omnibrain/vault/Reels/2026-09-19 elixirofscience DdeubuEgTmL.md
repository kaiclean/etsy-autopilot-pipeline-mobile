# Could one treatment ease anxiety for months In a Phase 3 trial involvi

## Caption
Could one treatment ease anxiety for months? In a Phase 3 trial involving 245 adults with generalized anxiety disorder, researchers tested a single dose of DT120. At week 12, anxiety scores had fallen by 9.8 points in the 100 µg group, compared with 4.7 points in the placebo group. Improvement was detected as early as day two, and 32% of participants receiving the drug had at least a 50% reduction in symptoms by week 12, versus 14% receiving placebo.

DT120 is an experimental, fast-dissolving formulation of lysergide (LSD), given under clinical supervision—not a medication to take at home. Perceptual effects, nausea and headache were common on the dosing day. An earlier randomized trial was published in JAMA, while these new Phase 3 figures are company-reported initial results awaiting full peer-reviewed publication and regulatory review. Still, the possibility of sustained relief after a single supervised treatment makes this an important development to watch.
