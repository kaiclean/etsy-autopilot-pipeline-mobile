# Swiss Budget and Debt-Free Planner CHF

## Problem
Swiss households face specific money pain points: Krankenkasse premiums, taxes billed in arrears, debt collection (Betreibung), and 3a pillar decisions. US budget templates don't fit: no CHF, no Steuer-Rückstellung, no Krankenkassen-Franchise logic. The debt reel shows huge interest in getting out of debt in Switzerland.

## Concept
'Budget Planer Schweiz' as a Google Sheets/Excel template + printable PDF in German (EN version later): monthly CHF budget with Swiss categories, a tax-reserve calculator, a Krankenkasse franchise comparison sheet, a debt snowball/avalanche tracker, a 'Betreibung? So gehst du vor' checklist pointing to free cantonal debt advice (Schuldenberatung Schweiz), plus savings goals and a 3a tracker.

## How to build it for real
1. Build the Google Sheet with formulas, protected ranges, a dashboard tab with charts; test it with real numbers.
2. Export Excel version; make a 12-page printable companion PDF.
3. Legal hygiene: informational only, no legal advice; link official sources (SchKG, schulden.ch).
4. Delivery: PDF with a 'Make a copy' link to the Sheet.
5. Listing in German ('Budgetplaner Schweiz CHF Google Sheets'), CHF 12-16; reel content: '4 Wege aus den Schulden in der Schweiz' explainer pointing to the template.

## Etsy fit
Strong niche: budget spreadsheets are an Etsy best-seller category, and a Swiss-specific CHF version faces almost no competition. Fits Designed by Kai's digital-download line and Swiss base.
