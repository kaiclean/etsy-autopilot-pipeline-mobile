# Swiss Admin Letter Templates

## Problem
People panic at Inkasso letters, Betreibungen and inflated fees and don't know how to respond formally. The Inkasso reel shows a letter template can be the whole fix.

## Concept
A Word/Google Docs pack of fill-in letters for Switzerland (and a Germany edition): dispute inflated collection fees, request an itemised statement, payment-plan request (Ratenzahlung/Stundung), Krankenkasse franchise change, cancellation letters (Abo/Handy/Fitness), Mietzins reduction request. Each comes with a one-page 'when to use / deadlines' guide.

## How to build it for real
1. Research the official wording and deadlines (e.g. 10-day Rechtsvorschlag in CH) from official sources (SchKG, Mieterverband, Konsumentenschutz), then get a jurist friend to review it once.
2. Write 12 templates in DE (CH spelling 'ss'), Word + Google Docs copies.
3. Clear disclaimer: templates, not legal advice.
4. Listing 'Vorlagen Briefe Schweiz' CHF 9-12; pairs with [[Swiss Budget and Debt-Free Planner CHF]] as a bundle.

## Etsy fit
Medium: low competition for Swiss-German letter templates. Smaller search volume, but a cheap bundle add-on.
