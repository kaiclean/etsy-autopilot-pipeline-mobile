# Recipe Card and Cookbook Template Kit

## Problem
Food creators and home bakers (like the '100 ways to make cheesecake' series) want consistent, beautiful recipe cards and series covers, but design tools are fiddly.

## Concept
An editable Canva kit: recipe cards (4×6, A5, A4), a family-cookbook template (40 pages), 'series' Instagram carousel templates for food creators (ingredients slide, steps slide, cover with episode number), and a German/Spanish/English unit-conversion cheat sheet.

## How to build it for real
1. Design 3 styles (minimal, bakery pastel, dark moody) in Canva.
2. Use the Biscoff cheesecake recipe structure as the demo content (rewritten, not copied).
3. Export template links + printable PDF; video mockup.
4. Price CHF 8-15; bundle with a printable baking-planner page.

## Etsy fit
Medium: recipe templates are evergreen sellers but competitive; the creator-carousel angle is the differentiator.
