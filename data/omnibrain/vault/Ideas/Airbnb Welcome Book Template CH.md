# Airbnb Welcome Book Template CH

## Problem
Swiss holiday-flat and Airbnb hosts (and the growing number of co-hosts managing several flats) need a guest welcome book, house rules, Wi-Fi and check-out signs, ideally in DE/EN/FR. Most Etsy templates are US-centric: wrong waste-separation rules, no Swiss quiet hours (Nachtruhe 22:00), no Tourist-Tax/Kurtaxe note, no SBB/public transport section.

## Concept
An editable Canva + PDF 'Swiss Host Kit': 30-page welcome book, house rules (Hausordnung, Abfalltrennung, Nachtruhe), Wi-Fi QR sign, check-in/out cards, local-tips pages, review-request card. Trilingual DE/EN/FR versions, 3 design styles (alpine minimal, modern, chalet). Upsell: done-for-you personalisation for CHF 49.

## How to build it for real
1. Collect real requirements: Swiss Hausordnung norms, Abfall/Recycling (PET, Alu, Karton, Gebührensack), Kurtaxe wording, emergency numbers 117/118/144/1414.
2. Design master in Canva (A5 + US Letter + phone-friendly digital version), 3 colour themes; write copy in DE, translate to EN/FR and proof-read.
3. Export: Canva template links PDF (delivered via Etsy digital file), plus a print-ready PDF sample.
4. Make 10 listing mockups (tablet, printed booklet on chalet table) + 1 short video flip-through for the listing and for OmniShop reels.
5. List 3 listings: full kit (CHF 19-24), signs-only pack (CHF 8), personalised service (CHF 49). Tags: airbnb welcome book, swiss, ferienwohnung, gästemappe, house rules sign.

## Etsy fit
Excellent: 'airbnb welcome book' is one of Etsy's evergreen digital best-sellers; the Swiss/German-language angle ('Gästemappe Ferienwohnung') is far less saturated and matches Kai's home market.
