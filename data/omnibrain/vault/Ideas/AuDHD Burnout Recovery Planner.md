# AuDHD Burnout Recovery Planner

## Problem
Neurodivergent people in burnout need structure that asks very little of them. Normal planners (daily schedules, habit streaks) feel like more obligations, and the reel's core insight is that rest alone doesn't fix AuDHD burnout.

## Concept
A low-demand printable + GoodNotes/Notability planner built on the reel's framework: 'do less', 'one safe person, one familiar place, one small good thing'. Includes an energy/spoon tracker, a stimulation-vs-overload balance page, a 'drop list' (what can I stop doing), a crash-pattern log, a 'next one action' card (the 'answer first, max 5 steps' style from the ADHD plugin reel), and a calm design with large tap targets.

## How to build it for real
1. Draft 25 page types; validate wording with neurodivergent communities and avoid any medical claims (add a disclaimer: not therapy).
2. Design in Affinity/Canva: printable A4/Letter PDF + hyperlinked digital planner PDF (tabs) for iPad.
3. Offer an undated version so nobody 'falls behind'.
4. Mockups: iPad with Apple Pencil, printed pages on a desk; 15s reel flipping through the pages for OmniShop.
5. Price CHF 9-14; bundle with a sticker sheet of low-energy reward stickers (digital PNG for GoodNotes).

## Etsy fit
Very strong: ADHD planners and digital GoodNotes planners are high-volume Etsy niches, and an 'AuDHD burnout' angle is specific enough to stand out. English first, German version second ('ADHS Planer').
