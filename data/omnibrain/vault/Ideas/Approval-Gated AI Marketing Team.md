# Approval-Gated AI Marketing Team

## Problem
Kai is a one-person shop. Research, ads, content, DMs and reporting don't scale, and fully autonomous posting is risky.

## Concept
OmniShop's operating model: specialised agents (Research: competitor top listings and ads; Creative: mockups/videos/captions; Community: comments/DMs; Analyst: daily one-page summary that ties reel → click → sale). Nothing goes live without Kai's tap. Budget caps on any paid ads.

## How to build it for real
1. Start with the Analyst: a daily 1-page digest (Etsy orders + visits + IG reach) delivered by Grok Bot, which partly exists already (analytics-kpi-digest).
2. Research agent: weekly scan of the top 20 competitor listings in each niche (public pages, polite rate), saved to OmniBrain.
3. Creative agent: generates 3 reel concepts + captions per new product; Kai approves in chat.
4. Community agent = [[Comment-to-DM Funnel for OmniShop]].
5. Only then consider Etsy Ads / Meta ads with a hard weekly CHF cap and automatic pause rules.

## Etsy fit
Multiplies output of a solo Etsy seller while keeping quality control.
