# Retro Gaming Hacker POD Collection

## Problem
Kai saves lots of console-hacking, emulation and browser-gaming content. That's a passionate subculture (emulation, homebrew, retro LAN), and most merch for it infringes trademarks.

## Concept
Trademark-safe POD designs for the homebrew/emulation crowd: 'It runs on Wine', 'Emulated, not pirated', 'Ping 999', 'Insert Coin to Continue', pixel-art controller blueprints, terminal-green 'sudo play' design, 'Tap to Pay? Not today' (NFC-security humour). Tees, hoodies, desk mats, stickers via Printify.

## How to build it for real
1. Brainstorm 30 slogans and filter out any Sony/Microsoft/Nintendo/Rockstar marks; check each phrase in the USPTO/EUIPO and Etsy search.
2. Design 8 in vector (Illustrator/Affinity), 2 colourways, Printify blueprints: Gildan 64000 tee, hoodie, desk mat, die-cut stickers.
3. Mockups + listings; reel format: 'POV: your PC games run on your phone now' with the shirt.
4. Track which slogans sell and expand the winners.

## Etsy fit
Medium: POD gamer merch is crowded, but niche hacker/emulation humour is underserved. Low effort with Kai's existing Printify pipeline.
