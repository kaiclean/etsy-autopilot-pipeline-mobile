# Cinematic LUT and Preset Pack

## Problem
Kai shot the camera work for this music video (credited @kai.leanhard). That dark, cinematic look is a sellable skill, and creators buy LUTs/presets to get a look like it.

## Concept
A 'Underneath' pack: 8-12 .cube LUTs for video (Premiere, DaVinci, CapCut, Final Cut) + matching Lightroom presets, built from the colour grade of the video and Kai's other footage. Before/after reels double as OmniShop content and Kai's portfolio.

## How to build it for real
1. Ask @upsi_kiwi (edit) for permission to use the grade/footage stills, and credit the collaborators.
2. Rebuild the grade in DaVinci Resolve, export .cube LUTs (33-point) and test on log + Rec709 footage from 3 cameras/phones.
3. Make Lightroom .xmp presets with matching tones.
4. Package a ZIP + install guide PDF; listing video with before/after splits.
5. Price CHF 12-19; cross-sell to music/creator audience on IG.

## Etsy fit
Good: LUTs and presets are popular Etsy digital goods; a genuine music-video origin story differentiates it.
